FastImpact Preach

An internal tool of Eglise Evangélique Internationale de Genève (EEIG GVA).

FastImpact Preach turns the church's recorded sermons into short vertical videos (YouTube Shorts, Reels) that highlight the teaching of the Gospel and of the Scriptures. It transcribes a sermon, suggests meaningful passages, adds subtitles, and — when a team member asks for it — publishes the selected shorts to the church's own YouTube channel.

Who can use it

Only members of the EEIG GVA media team, with a team password. It is not a public service.

How YouTube is used

  • A team member connects the church's YouTube channel with Google sign-in and explicitly authorises uploads.
  • The app only uploads the videos the team member selects and confirms, with their title and description.
  • It reads the channel name to show which channel will receive the videos. It does not read, change or delete anything else on the channel.
