Privacy policy

FastImpact Preach · Eglise Evangélique Internationale de Genève (EEIG GVA) · last updated 7 October 2026

This policy explains what FastImpact Preach does with information when the EEIG GVA media team uses it, including data obtained from Google through YouTube API Services.

1. Information we access

  • Sermon videos uploaded or linked by the team, their transcripts and the short videos produced from them.
  • Google / YouTube account data, only after a team member signs in with Google and grants access: the YouTube channel name and channel ID, and OAuth access and refresh tokens. The permissions requested are youtube.upload (to upload the selected shorts) and youtube.readonly (to display the channel name).
  • For other networks the team may connect (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok): the page/account name and its tokens.

2. How we use it

  • To upload the short videos that a team member selects and explicitly confirms, to the channel they chose.
  • To show which channel is connected and to confirm that an upload is online.

We do not use Google user data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than these features, and we do not sell or transfer it to anyone. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Storage and security

  • Data is stored on the church's own server hosted by Infomaniak in Switzerland, behind HTTPS and a team password.
  • OAuth tokens are kept in a file readable only by the application, and are never shown in the interface.
  • Sermon transcripts are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to suggest passages; no Google user data is sent to it.

4. Retention and deletion

  • Tokens are kept until the team disconnects the account in FastImpact Preach (Publish to → Disconnect / Remove), which deletes them immediately.
  • You can also revoke access at any time on the Google security settings page (security.google.com/settings/security/permissions).
  • Projects and videos are kept until the team deletes them in the app. On request, we delete all stored data within 30 days.

5. YouTube

FastImpact Preach uses YouTube API Services. By using it to publish to YouTube you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
